## About

Feed & Seed is a small web and graphic design studio. We work with local businesses in and around Kinderhook, NY — farms, markets, shops, restaurants, and the people running them.

The studio is run by **David Nyman**, working out of a barn in the village. Feed & Seed is one working part of **[Harmonic Systems](https://harmonic-systems.org)**, the broader practice David runs — design, software, and field research that prefers analog-first methods and quiet, useful tools.

### What we believe

- **Simple sites do the work of complicated ones, faster.** A clean page with the right information beats a fancy site that's slow to load and hard to update.
- **Local businesses deserve to be found.** Whether someone is typing into Google, asking ChatGPT, or saying "Hey Siri, where can I get…" — your business should show up.
- **Technology should serve the experience, not dominate it.** We use the smallest amount of code that does the job. Static sites, plain HTML, real content.
- **Respect the reader.** No pop-ups, no auto-playing video, no harvesting strangers' attention.

### How we work

We start with a conversation. We learn what your business does, who comes through the door, and what you'd like more of. Then we build the smallest, sturdiest thing that gets that done — usually a single-page or few-page site, indexed cleanly, easy to update.

After it ships, we make sure it's findable. That means structured data (the small machine-readable labels that tell search engines and AI assistants what your business *is*), local-search signals (where you are, what you do, who you serve), and clean content that answers the questions people actually ask. See [how we make sites visible to AI search]({{ '/geo' | relative_url }}) for the methodology in detail.
