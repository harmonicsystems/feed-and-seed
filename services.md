## Consultation FAQ

**What is a consultation?**  
A conversation. We sit down (or call) and figure out what your business actually needs. No jargon, no upsell.

**What do you need from me to get started?**  
Just show up. If you have a logo, a menu, a photo of your storefront, or even a napkin sketch — bring it. If you have nothing, that's fine too.

**How much does a website cost?**  
It depends on what you need. A single-page site for a farm stand is different from a full site with online ordering. We'll scope it together during a consultation.

**Do I need a website if I already have social media?**  
Probably, yes — and here's why. When someone asks Google, ChatGPT, or Siri "where can I buy local honey near Kinderhook," those tools pull answers from websites, not Instagram posts. This is called Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — making sure AI-powered search tools can find and recommend your business.

Social media is great for regulars, but it's a walled garden. Your posts don't show up in search results the way a proper webpage does. A simple site with your name, what you sell, where you are, and when you're open puts you on the map — literally. Without one, you're invisible to a growing number of people who search by asking a question instead of scrolling a feed.

The good news: the site doesn't need to be complicated. A single page with the right information, structured clearly, does more for discoverability than a year of Instagram posts.

**Can you work with my existing site?**  
Usually, yes. Sometimes it makes more sense to start fresh. We'll be honest about which.

**How long does a project take?**  
A simple site can be up in a week or two. Print jobs are usually faster. We'll give you a timeline upfront.

**Do you handle hosting and domains?**  
Yes. We can set it all up and manage it, or hand you the keys if you prefer.

**What if I just need a flyer for one event?**  
That's fine. Not everything has to be a big project.
